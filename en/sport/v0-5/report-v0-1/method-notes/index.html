Target definition

future_goal_h10

future_goal_h10 is positive when a goal occurs within the next 10 event steps after the evaluated event. The top-10 lift compares the positive rate in the top-ranked 10% slice with the base-rate reference. The baseline line in the validation figure is therefore normalized to 1.0.

Hybrid score glossary

What the formula terms mean.

hybrid_score_v0_1_raw = 0.50 * event_risk_calibrated + 0.05 * inside_hardened_chain + 0.10 * chain_delta_context + 0.35 * subtype_reliability
TermType / scaleDefinition
event_risk_calibratedcontinuous, 0-1Normalized calibrated event-level risk estimate.
inside_hardened_chainbinary, 0/1Event belongs to a filtered and stabilized chain pattern.
chain_delta_contextcontinuous, 0-1Normalized local change in risk inside the selected event window.
subtype_reliabilitycontinuous, 0-1Reliability weight attached to the event subtype in the validation setup.

Chain-card metrics

Risk peak and risk delta.

risk_peak is the maximum normalized hybrid score inside the selected chain-card window. risk_delta is the local increase between the lower-risk part of the selected window and the peak-risk point. These values are normalized scores, not direct scoring probabilities.

Robustness labels

Robust vs promising.

Robust

A stronger public demonstration subtype that passed the current rule/filter layer with stable enough evidence for illustrative use.

Promising

A useful product example that should be expanded with more cross-domain evidence before being treated as stable.
